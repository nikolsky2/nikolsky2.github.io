Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026

This policy explains how gotBottle processes information when you use its iOS and Android applications.

Policy summary

This privacy policy explains how gotBottle collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you use our mobile application.

What information we collect:

  • Usage Data: Information about how you use the app, including app interactions and feature usage
  • Device Information: Device identifiers, operating system, and app version
  • Location Data: Location information when you use location-based features (only when permitted). Depending on your device settings and platform, this may be precise or approximate.
  • Camera Access: Camera access for photo-taking features (only when permitted)
  • Crash Data: Technical information about app crashes to improve stability

How we use your information:

  • To provide and improve app functionality
  • To analyze app usage and performance
  • To fix bugs and improve app stability
  • To provide location-based features when requested
  • To process OCR text from wine labels through Google Firebase AI to provide AI-powered wine analysis

What we do NOT share:

  • Sales and advertising: We do not sell personal information or use it for cross-context behavioral advertising.
  • Collection content: We do not host your bottle collection or backup files. Your bottles, ratings, notes, and photos remain on your device unless you choose to export or share them.

Third-party services we use:

  • Google Analytics for Firebase: App analytics and usage tracking
  • Firebase Crashlytics: Crash reporting and app stability monitoring
  • Google Firebase AI: Processing text from wine label photos to provide AI-powered wine analysis
  • Subscription and mapping providers: Firebase Authentication and Cloud Functions, Google Play, Google Maps Platform and Google Play services on Android, and Apple location and mapping services on iOS

Your rights:

You have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of your personal data. You can also withdraw consent at any time and lodge complaints with data protection authorities.

Detailed information on the processing of Personal Data

Personal Data is collected for the following purposes and using the following services:

Analytics

The services in this section help us understand App use, diagnose problems, and maintain reliability.

Google Analytics for Firebase (Google LLC)

Firebase Analytics is an analytics service provided by Google LLC that helps us understand how users interact with our app. To understand Google's use of data, please review Google's Privacy & Terms.

Firebase Analytics processes app usage patterns, user interactions, device and app information, and Firebase technical identifiers. Android subscription analytics may include a pseudonymous Firebase user identifier, product information, a masked purchase-token fragment, and purchase or validation status.

Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics are enabled to measure usage and diagnose reliability problems. The App does not currently offer an in-app analytics opt-out.

Advertising settings, including iOS tracking settings, do not by themselves disable Firebase Analytics collection. You can stop further App-side collection by uninstalling the App, subject to data already received and retained by the relevant provider.

Data collected: Usage data, app interactions, device and app information, Firebase technical identifiers, and technical performance data.

Place of processing: United States and other Google data centers worldwide - Google Privacy Policy. Google participates in data transfer frameworks including Standard Contractual Clauses and adequacy decisions.

Subscription validation

Android subscriptions are validated through Firebase Authentication and Firebase Cloud Functions using Google Play purchase data. This processing uses a pseudonymous Firebase user identifier, product ID, purchase token, transaction or order information, and subscription entitlement status.

The information is used to confirm purchases, prevent fraud, provide entitlement support, and meet legal obligations. Google Play, Firebase, and Google Cloud process this information under their applicable terms and privacy notices.

Firebase Crashlytics (Google LLC)

Firebase Crashlytics is a crash reporting service provided by Google LLC that helps us identify and fix app crashes and stability issues. Crashlytics automatically collects crash reports, including stack traces and device information, when the app encounters an error.

This service helps us improve app reliability by providing detailed crash reports that allow us to identify and resolve issues quickly.

Data collected: Crash logs, stack traces, device model, operating system version, app version, and timestamp of crashes.

Place of processing: United States and other Google data centers worldwide - Google Privacy Policy. Google uses appropriate safeguards for international data transfers.

Google Firebase AI (Google LLC)

Firebase AI Logic using the Vertex AI backend processes text extracted from wine-label photos when you choose AI-powered bottle analysis. This enables the App to provide a suggested wine profile, tasting notes, and recommendations.

Important: The original label photo and your tasting notes are not sent for this request. The OCR text, language and locale information, and service metadata needed to process the request may be sent to the service. Do not use the feature for labels or images containing sensitive personal information.

Data collected: OCR text extracted from wine-label photos, language and locale information, and service metadata needed to process the request.

Place of processing: United States and other Google data centers worldwide - Google Privacy Policy.
